# Chalkfy

A macOS app that a presenter uses to point at things on a shared screen during a live call.

## Language

**Presenter**:
The person who shares their screen in a call and runs Chalkfy.
_Avoid_: user, speaker

**Audience**:
The people in the call who watch the presenter's shared screen.
_Avoid_: viewers, participants

**Overlay**:
The transparent layer above every app, on every display, where Chalkfy draws its effects.

**Highlight**:
A mode where a ring follows the cursor so the audience can find it.
_Avoid_: cursor ring, halo, spotlight

**Laser**:
A mode where the presenter drags the mouse to draw a red stroke that fades by itself. The drag does not reach the app below.
_Avoid_: laser pointer, pen, annotation
